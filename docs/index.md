## About me

Hi! I'm Richard, a technical founder from the Netherlands, based in Lisbon.

For the past 12 years I've been building and running
[Blendergrid](https://blendergrid.com), a render farm that runs on a network
of 30+ datacenters around the world. Along the way I learned that the best way
to understand something is to build it yourself.

That's what I do on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@richardvdoost) and in
my [articles](articles/index.md): I build things from scratch in C, from a
matrix library to unit testing to a neural network, and explain every step.

Want 1:1 help with your code, or does your company have a project? See
[how to work with me](work/index.md).
