### Frequently asked questions

??? note "How quickly can you start working on my project?"
    I can typically begin new projects within 1-2 weeks of contract signing.
    For urgent matters I keep some flexibility and can potentially start
    sooner. Just let me know your timeline during the intro call.

??? note "Do you require a minimum project size or commitment?"
    I can accommodate projects of any size, but engagements of at least 20
    hours allow for meaningful impact. That gives us enough time to understand
    your situation, implement solutions and deliver results. We can start with
    a small pilot project to make sure we're a good fit.

??? note "How do you handle data security and confidentiality?"
    I sign NDAs before starting any project, encrypt all data transfers and
    follow industry best practices for data handling. I can also work within
    your existing security infrastructure and policies.

??? note "How do you communicate progress and results?"
    You get weekly progress updates and regular check-in meetings, plus
    documentation of all analyses, findings and recommendations.
